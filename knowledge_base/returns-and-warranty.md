# Returns and warranty

## Returns
Orders are sold and fulfilled through Amazon, so a return starts in the customer's Amazon account under Your Orders, within 30 days of delivery.

Amazon issues the return label and handles the refund.

Harbor & Pine cannot start a return, speed one up, approve one, or issue a refund.

## Warranty
Paddle boards — 2 years on the board, seams and valve, 1 year on the accessories.

Shelving — 2 years on panels and hardware.

The warranty covers manufacturing faults only. Damage from use is not covered, for example a hull punctured on a rock or a panel marked during assembly.

## Other products
Every other Harbor & Pine product follows the same rules: a 30-day Amazon return window and a 2-year warranty against manufacturing faults.
