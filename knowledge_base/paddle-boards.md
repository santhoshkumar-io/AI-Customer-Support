# Paddle boards

## Sizes
Three inflatable boards are available:

- 10'6" All-round — riders up to 85 kg.
- 11' All-round — riders up to 110 kg. The most stable of the three.
- 12'6" Touring — riders up to 130 kg. Fastest in a straight line.

A rider who falls between two sizes should take the larger board.

## What is in the box
Every board ships with:

- Board (PB-BRD-106, PB-BRD-110 or PB-BRD-126 depending on size)
- 3-piece adjustable paddle (PB-PDL-3)
- Hand pump with pressure gauge (PB-PMP-1)
- Removable centre fin (PB-FIN-1)
- Coiled leash (PB-LSH-1)
- Valve wrench (PB-VLV-W)
- Carry bag (PB-BAG-1)

Accessories are ordered by part number.

## Inflation
Inflate to between 12 and 15 PSI. Below 12 PSI the board bends in the middle and feels unstable underfoot.

Losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight is normal. Air cools and contracts, and the reading drops without any air escaping.

## Valve check for slow leaks
Run this before treating a board as faulty:

1. Inflate to 15 PSI.
2. Brush soapy water around the valve and watch for bubbles.
3. Fit the valve wrench and turn the valve core a quarter turn clockwise.
4. Leave the board 24 hours, then check the pressure again.

If the board still loses more than 2 PSI over 24 hours, collect the order number, board size and delivery date.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment.

- Missing accessory — the part is shipped.
- Slow leak — the valve check above comes first.
- Leak continuing after the valve check — a new board within 30 days of delivery, or a patch kit after that.
- Split seam — a colleague takes over.
