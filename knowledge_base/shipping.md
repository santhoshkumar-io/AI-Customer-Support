# Shipping

## Orders
Amazon ships every order and owns the tracking, the carrier and the delivery date.

Harbor & Pine cannot see an Amazon delivery, change its address, or move its delivery date. Tracking for an order lives in the customer's Amazon account.

## Replacement parts
Replacement parts and hardware kits ship free from the Harbor & Pine warehouse.

They leave the warehouse within 2 business days and arrive in 3 to 7 business days.

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves.

## Countries
Products are sold wherever they are listed on Amazon, and Amazon decides which countries that covers.

Replacement parts are sent to the address on the original order.
