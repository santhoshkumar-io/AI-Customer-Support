# Shelving

## Models and finishes
Four flat-pack models:

- HP-S3 — 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm tall.
- HP-S5 — 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm tall.
- HP-C4 — 2x2 cube unit, 77 cm tall.
- HP-C9 — 3x3 cube unit, 112 cm tall.

Each model comes in natural oak, white, black and walnut.

The model code is printed on the assembly manual and on the shipping box.

## Parts lists
Every panel carries a numbered sticker. A part number is the model code plus the panel number, for example HP-S3-02.

- HP-S3 — 2 side panels (01), 1 top panel (02), 3 shelves (03), 1 back panel (04), hardware kit HK-S.
- HP-S5 — 2 side panels (01), 1 top panel (02), 5 shelves (03), 1 back panel (04), hardware kit HK-S.
- HP-C4 — 2 side panels (01), 1 top panel (02), 1 base panel (03), 1 shelf (04), 1 divider (05), 1 back panel (06), hardware kit HK-C.
- HP-C9 — 2 side panels (01), 1 top panel (02), 1 base panel (03), 2 shelves (04), 2 dividers (05), 1 back panel (06), hardware kit HK-C.

Hardware kits are HK-S for the S models and HK-C for the C models.

## Assembly tips
- Lay the panels on carpet or cardboard so the finish is not scratched.
- Turn each cam lock until it stops, about half a turn. Forcing it further strips the panel.
- The back panel goes on with its finished side facing into the shelf.
- Fix the tall units, HP-S5 and HP-C9, to the wall using the anchor in the hardware kit.
- One person needs 30 to 60 minutes.

## Problems and what happens next
- Missing hardware — the hardware kit for that model is shipped.
- One or two damaged panels — the panels are shipped by part number.
- Three or more damaged panels, or a damaged frame — a full replacement unit.
- Wrong finish delivered — exchanged for the finish ordered.

Collect the order number, the model, the damaged part numbers and a photo where the customer can send one.
